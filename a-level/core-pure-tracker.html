RAG key

How the scoring works

🔴

1 — Red

Needs work. The student can't yet do this skill independently and should revisit it.

🟡

2 — Amber

Partial. The student can do this with support or makes some errors — nearly there.

🟢

3 — Green

Confident. The student can do this skill reliably and unaided.

How to use it

Three steps

①

Add your class

Type your students' names across the top row, replacing the sample names.

②

Score as you teach

After each topic, enter 1, 2 or 3 for each skill and student. Cells colour automatically.

③

Read the summaries

Each topic shows a class-average row, and every student gets an overall confidence figure.

💡 Topics covered: Complex Numbers · Argand Diagrams · Series · Roots of Polynomials · Volumes of Revolution · Matrices · Linear Transformations · Proof by Induction · Vectors. Based on the Edexcel Core Pure 1 specification.
