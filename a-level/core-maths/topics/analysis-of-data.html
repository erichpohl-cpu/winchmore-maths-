3.1 · Paper 1 · Compulsory

Analysis of Data

Averages, spread, diagrams and how to read real data critically. The bedrock of the whole course.

Specification content

What you need to know

Click any sub-topic to see the full list of things you must be able to do. This follows the AQA 1350 specification.

3.1.1

Types of data & sampling

▾
  • Categorical, discrete and continuous data
  • Primary vs secondary data
  • Populations, samples, census
  • Random, systematic, stratified, quota and opportunity sampling
  • Advantages and disadvantages of each sampling method
3.1.2

Diagrams & representation

▾
  • Frequency tables and grouped frequency tables
  • Bar charts, pie charts, pictograms
  • Stem-and-leaf diagrams
  • Cumulative frequency curves
  • Box-and-whisker plots (box plots)
  • Histograms with unequal class widths and frequency density
3.1.3

Measures of central tendency

▾
  • Mean, median and mode from lists and tables
  • Estimated mean from grouped data
  • Choosing the most appropriate average
  • Effect of outliers and changes to data
3.1.4

Measures of spread

▾
  • Range and interquartile range (IQR)
  • Percentiles and deciles from cumulative frequency
  • Standard deviation using the formula and calculator
  • Variance
  • Using the mean & standard deviation to compare distributions
3.1.5

Outliers & comparison

▾
  • Identifying outliers using 1.5 × IQR rule
  • Identifying outliers using mean ± 2 standard deviations
  • Comparing two data sets in context
  • Skewness (positive, negative, symmetrical) from diagrams
