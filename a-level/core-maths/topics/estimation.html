3.3 · Paper 1 · Compulsory

Estimation

Fermi estimation, sensible approximations and sanity-checking answers. Short unit, easy marks if practised.

Specification content

What you need to know

Click any sub-topic to see the full list of things you must be able to do. This follows the AQA 1350 specification.

3.3.1

Approximation techniques

▾
  • Rounding to significant figures and decimal places
  • Estimating calculations using 1 significant figure
  • Checking answers are of the right order of magnitude
3.3.2

Fermi estimation

▾
  • Making and justifying assumptions
  • Multi-step 'back of envelope' estimates
  • Estimating quantities that can't be measured directly (e.g. piano tuners in a city)
  • Stating assumptions clearly and evaluating their effect
3.3.3

Estimation in context

▾
  • Estimating areas and volumes of irregular shapes
  • Estimating populations and totals from samples
  • Judging whether an estimate is an over- or under-estimate
