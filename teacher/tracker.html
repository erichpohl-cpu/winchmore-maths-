Step 1

Download the tracker

One workbook with a Class list tab and a tab for each chapter. Type names once on the Class list; every chapter tab fills in automatically.

↓ Download the workbook (.xlsx)
📋 On each chapter tab, score every pupil 1, 2 or 3 (1 = red / needs work, 2 = amber / partial, 3 = green / confident). Cells colour themselves as you type.
📝 Two ways to fill it in. Either mark the workbook yourself, or set a Google Form as a reflective task in Classroom and let pupils self-assess privately — then download the responses as CSV and upload that instead. See open the Create Forms script to generate the Forms (one per course; pupils skip topics not yet taught).

Step 2

Upload for a report

Drop your completed workbook here, or a Google Form responses CSV. Blank / unanswered skills count as “not seen”. Everything runs locally in this tab.
